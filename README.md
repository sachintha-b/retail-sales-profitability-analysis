# retail-sales-profitability-analysis
Developed an interactive Tableau dashboard to diagnose profitability across products, customer segments, regions, discounts, and returns; identified key sources of margin erosion and translated findings into business recommendations.

# Retail Sales & Profitability Diagnostic Dashboard

## Introduction

An analytical dashboard created in Tableau to assess retail sales,
profitability, customer segments, product performance, regional
trends, discounts, and product returns.

It demonstrates the key factors contributing to the decline in profits
and provides recommendations based on the findings.

## Executive Summary

This dashboard allows users to explore and identify key factors that
impact business performance, particularly profitability. It visualizes
insights into the main issues and provides recommendations based on
the analysis.

### Business Questions

- What are the most profitable and least profitable areas of the business?
- What is the impact of discounts on profitability?
- Which products and product categories are the most profitable?
- What are the most common reasons for returns?
- Which customers and regions need special attention?

The analysis revealed that despite revenue exceeding $2.0M, the company
experienced losses of approximately $28.8K. Profitability was negatively
affected by significant discounts, product returns, and the concentration
of losses across several products, customer segments, regions, and
city-category combinations.

In particular, the Corporate and Home Office segments generated
significant revenue but experienced losses.

This dashboard uses Tableau to create an interactive tool for addressing
business questions through calculated fields, KPIs, and various
visualizations. The analysis involved several calculations to examine
discounts, returns, profitability, and other factors.
